Halflife

Halflife is an iPhone app that tracks caffeine the way your body actually processes it. Photograph a drink and it is logged. Tap a widget, the Action Button, or Back Tap to log your usual. The app learns your personal caffeine half-life from your check-ins and your sleep, then tells you when your peak focus window is, when to have your last coffee, and how much caffeine will still be circulating at bedtime.

Everything runs on your iPhone. There is no account and no server.
