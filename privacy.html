Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Halflife is built so that your data stays on your iPhone. The app has no account system, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising, and no third‑party software development kits. This policy explains what the app stores, what it reads from Apple Health, and what Apple handles on our behalf.

What the app stores on your device

  • Drink log. Each drink you log (name, caffeine amount, time opened and finished), your favorite drinks, and drink groups.
  • Check‑ins and calibration. Your answers to “how do you feel” check‑ins and the caffeine half‑life the app learns from them.
  • Profile. The details you enter to seed the model: age, weight, sensitivity, bedtime and wake time, and yes/no answers about smoking, oral contraceptives, pregnancy, and CYP1A2‑inhibiting medication.
  • Drink photos. If you attach a photo to a logged drink it is saved as a file inside the app’s private container on your phone. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Barcode memory. When you scan a barcode, the app remembers which drink it matched so the next scan is instant. This is a local lookup table only.

This data lives in an App Group container shared only between the Halflife app and its own widgets, Control Center control, and Live Activity on the same device. It is included in your normal iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have backups enabled. You can export it as JSON or delete all of it at any time from Profile → Data → Reset everything.

Camera and photos

Drink recognition (barcode reading, text reading, and image classification) runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple’s on‑device Vision framework. Camera frames and photos you pick are processed in memory and are not sent anywhere. Photos are stored only if you choose to attach one to a drink.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health, Halflife:

  • Writes each drink you log to Health as dietary caffeine.
  • Reads sleep analysis, heart rate, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability. These are used on your device to calibrate your personal half‑life and your sleep‑safe caffeine threshold.

Health data is never transmitted off the device, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Halflife.

Notifications

Reminders (peak focus window, last call, bedtime load, daily limit, worn off) are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Subscriptions and payment

Halflife Pro is sold as an auto‑renewing subscription through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds your billing details; we never see your card number or Apple Account details. The app verifies your subscription status on device using Apple’s StoreKit framework. The only subscription information stored by the app is whether Pro is currently active.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not operate a server that receives data from the app.

Children

Halflife is not directed at children under 13 and the profile requires an age of 13 or older.
