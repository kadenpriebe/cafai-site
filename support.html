Subscription help

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Halflife. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restoring a purchase

On the plan screen tap “Restore purchases” while signed in to the Apple Account that made the original purchase.
